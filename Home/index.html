Understanding Your Home Loan & Floating Rates

Most home loans in India are linked to an external benchmark like the RBI Repo Rate. When rates change or when you make partial prepayments, your principal and tenure adjust automatically.

Principal

The amount borrowed. Every extra rupee you prepay directly reduces this balance.

Repo Rate Resets

When interest rates drop, a larger portion of your existing EMI goes toward principal, closing your loan faster.

FY Planning

Grouping your loan by Financial Year (Apr–Mar) makes it easy to track annual principal & interest for tax deductions.

The Power of Prepayment: Save Lakhs!

On a ₹50 Lakh loan for 20 years at 8.5% interest, simply paying one extra EMI each year saves over ₹9.3 Lakhs in interest and closes your loan 3 years and 2 months early!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the Floating Rate Simulator work?

Add any future month number and the new interest rate. From that month onward, monthly interest is calculated using the new rate while keeping your regular EMI steady—mirroring how banks adjust your remaining tenure.

Are there any prepayment charges in India?

As per RBI guidelines, banks and HFCs cannot levy prepayment penalties or foreclosure charges on floating-rate home loans taken by individuals for non-business purposes.

How do I share my custom plan?

Click "Share Plan Link" at the top. It encodes all your inputs, custom prepayments, and rate changes into a link you can bookmark or send on WhatsApp.

After prepaying, reduce EMI or tenure?

Always opt to keep your EMI unchanged and reduce the loan tenure. This maximizes interest savings and gets you debt-free much faster.
